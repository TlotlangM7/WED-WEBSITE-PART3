Welcome to Moeng Farms

Moeng Farms is a national farming business established in Limpopo in 1919 by Mr Moses Mmileng and Miss B Johnson. The farm supplies fruits, vegetables and meat to different sectors, from wholesalers to small local vendor shops.

Our promise: quality products, good service and fresh produce handled with love.
