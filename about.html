About Moeng Farms

Moeng Farms is a national farming business established in Limpopo in 1919 by Mr Moses Mmileng and Miss B Johnson, two cousins. The farm supplies fruits, vegetables and meat to all sectors of the market, from large wholesalers to small local vendor shops.

The farm was previously run by the Young Van Tonder family from 1919 to 1941. The Moeng family took over in 1941 and has more than 40 years of experience since that takeover, continuing to grow the farm and business to a national level.

Moeng Farms motto: “If it's not farm fresh then it's not Moeng.”

Mission

Moeng Farms believes in sustainable farming methods, using fewer chemicals and quality natural resources to reduce pollution and support the environment. The farm also prioritises fresh, quality produce and meat.

Vision

To be part of customers' food journeys by making fresh produce and meat a priority, whether supplying meat to a restaurant or fruits and vegetables to a local vendor.
